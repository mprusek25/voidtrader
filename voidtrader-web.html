MISSION PARAMETERS

SYS-01
STELLAR SYSTEMS

Each sector contains a procedural star system — a central star with 3–8 planets in Kepler orbits. Land on planets that have orbital stations, trade between solar systems light-years apart.

G · K · M · A · B · F STARS
SYS-02
CONTRACT SYSTEM

Five contract types — Courier, Freight, Express, Supply and Contraband runs — scale pay with distance in light-years. No pre-buying needed for courier jobs; just fly there and collect.

2,000–15,000 CR / RUN
SYS-03
LASER COMBAT

Fire in controlled bursts — energy depletes, heat builds. Overheat and the cannon locks for 2.2 seconds. Pirates avoid safe zones near stations; open space is lawless.

OVERHEAT MECHANIC
SYS-04
REAL-SCALE DISTANCES

1 game unit = 1 light-second. Planetary orbits at true AU scale. Jump between star systems measured in light-years. HUD shows ls / kls / ly dynamically.

1 AU = 499 ls
SYS-05
PROC-GEN GALAXY

Seed-based generation creates a deterministic Milky Way — every system, planet, station and price consistent between sessions. Your universe saves automatically.

ENDLESS WORLD
SYS-06
DISTRESS & SALVAGE

Run dry on fuel? Broadcast a distress beacon — rescue arrives but pirates follow the signal. Die and respawn with cargo jettisoned as retrievable drops in space.
